# Pulse Pair

An intimate, dark mobile-first pairing demo. Its visual language is deep blue-black, pale cyan light, restrained typography, and a single animated signal orb. The UI should feel like a calm moment of physical proximity rather than a generic dashboard.

Use the serif display face only for the large emotional headlines. Keep all task actions direct and legible, with high contrast, generous tap targets, and concise explanation of prototype/security limits.
